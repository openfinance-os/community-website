# CBUAE Open Finance — Annexure 1: Timelines for Market Adoption

**Source:** CBUAE official "Annexure 1: Timelines for Market Adoption of Open Finance", **Updated as of January 2026** (provided by Michael, 22 Jun 2026).
**Authority:** This is the authoritative CBUAE mandate calendar + LFI tier categorization. Supersedes any approximate dates/tiers used elsewhere in the watch tooling.

Legend: **M** = Mandatory · **O** = Optional (per LFI policy) · **N/A** = Not Applicable.
Two go-live columns: **Tier 1** and **Tier 2** "Production Environment & Go-Live Readiness Deadline".

## E. Reconciliation notes for the watch tooling

- **WIO Bank is Tier 2**, not Tier 1 — yet it is one of the highest-volume LFIs in the Nebras sandbox logs (a Tier-2 bank running ahead of its Sep-2026 deadline). ADCB is **Tier 1 (#3)**.
- Of the 10 LFI keys seen in the public logs: **Tier 1** = FAB, Emirates NBD, ADCB, DIB, Mashreq, ADIB, HSBC (retail + wholesale = HSBC Bank Middle East), CBD. **Tier 2** = WIO Bank. (Other Tier-2 banks and all insurers are not yet in the logs.)
- This annexure has **no standalone "FX & Remittance" milestone** — FX/international payments are folded into *International Payments* (Retail/SME/Corporate Dec-2025 Tier 1; SME bulk/intl Mar-2026 Tier 1). The watch radar's "FX & Remittance Q2-2026" row should be re-mapped accordingly.
- **Open Insurance Tier-1 data+quotes deadline is Feb-2026** (already passed), Tier-2 March-2027 — replaces the approximate "Insurance Suite Q2-2026".
- **V2.2 Final** uplift is the next standards milestone (Tier 1 Dec-2026 / Tier 2 Feb-2027 banking; same for insurance Tier 1 Dec-2026 / Tier 2 March-2027) — watch for V2.2 publication "shortly".
