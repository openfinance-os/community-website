Introduction

OpenFinance-OS is the community home for UAE Open Finance. This brand guide ensures consistency across all touchpoints, building recognition and trust within the ecosystem.

About This Guide

These guidelines define how we present OpenFinance-OS visually and verbally. Following them ensures our brand remains cohesive, professional, and recognizable.

Key Principle

OpenFinance-OS is a community initiative, not an official regulatory body. Our brand must clearly communicate this while maintaining professionalism expected in financial services.

For Contributors

Community members creating content, documentation, or materials for OpenFinance-OS.

For Partners

Organizations referencing or co-branding with OpenFinance-OS in their materials.

02

Mission & Values

Our mission and values guide every decision we make, from design choices to content creation.

Our Mission

"To bring visibility, resources, and collaboration to the UAE Open Finance ecosystem through community-driven, open-source tools and content."

Core Values

🌐 Transparency

We operate in the open. Our code, data sources, and decision-making are visible to all.

🤝 Collaboration

We believe ecosystems thrive when participants work together.

🔗 Complementary

We link to official sources, never replace them.

⚖️ Neutral

We don't rank or favor specific participants. Our content is factual and balanced.

05

Logo Usage Guidelines

Proper logo usage ensures brand consistency. Follow these guidelines in all applications.

Clear Space & Minimum Sizes

Maintain minimum clear space around the logo equal to the height of the accent dot. Never use the logo smaller than 32px for digital or 0.5" for print.

Context Minimum Size Notes
Digital 32px Use simplified favicon below 48px
Print 0.5" / 12mm 300dpi minimum resolution
Full logo 200px width Use mark only if narrower

Usage Rules

✓ Do

  • Use adequate clear space
  • Use dark version on dark backgrounds
  • Maintain aspect ratio
  • Use provided color variations

✗ Don't

  • Stretch or distort
  • Change colors or gradients
  • Add effects not specified
  • Place on busy backgrounds

Voice & Tone

Our voice is consistent; our tone adapts to context. Here's how we communicate.

Writing Principles

Be Clear

Use plain language. Avoid jargon. If technical terms are necessary, explain them.

Be Helpful

Anticipate questions. Provide context. Link to resources.

Be Honest

Never overstate our authority. Clarify our community nature.

Be Inclusive

Welcome all skill levels. Avoid assumptions about knowledge.

The community home for UAE Open Finance
