# Two sites, one ecosystem — a proposed division of labour

**From:** OpenFinance-OS (openfinance-os.org) — independent, community-run
**To:** Nebras Open Finance
**Re:** How our two UAE Open Finance sites can complement rather than duplicate each other

---

## The situation

Both nebras-open-finance.com and openfinance-os.org serve the UAE Open Finance community, and today they overlap — similar messaging, both with an "ecosystem dashboard," both with participant listings and learning content. That's confusing for visitors and duplicated effort for both teams.

We think there's a clean, durable split — one that plays to each site's structural strength rather than having us compete.

## The proposed split

| | **nebras-open-finance.com** — the operator hub | **openfinance-os.org** — the independent observatory |
|---|---|---|
| **Owns** | Building & operating on Al Tareq | Understanding, tracking & comparing the ecosystem |
| **Content** | API specs, TPP standards, LFI integration, certification, service desk, live program metrics | Registry record over time, global/regulatory comparisons, community commons, plain-language on-ramp, independent analysis |
| **Authority from** | Being *inside* the program | Being *independent* of it |

The logic: an operator can't credibly be the neutral scorekeeper of its own program — and a community project can't credibly be the authoritative source for how to integrate. Each of us is strongest exactly where the other structurally can't be.

## What we've already done on our side

We've repositioned openfinance-os.org to stop competing with you and start pointing to you:

- Our hero now leads with **"the independent record"**, not "tools for building."
- Our integration guides (TPP / LFI / API) are now **short stubs that link to your canonical docs** rather than mirroring them.
- Our FAQ and getting-started route all operational questions to your `/tech/*` pages.
- Our About page carries an explicit **"Building? → Nebras. Tracking/comparing? → us."** signpost.

The overlap is already mostly gone from our side.

## Three things that would make this work better

1. **Bless the data feeds as an open interface.**
   Our weekly Ecosystem Watch already reads your public `/api/*.json` feeds. We'd love for that to be an *intentional* open-data feed rather than an implementation detail — we'll archive it daily and build the long-term independent record and analysis on top. You generate the data (only you can); we keep the neutral archive and analysis (only we credibly can). Our daily archive also protects you: if you ever narrow the window, the history is preserved with us.

2. **Keep your canonical doc URLs stable.**
   Our Learn stubs deep-link into your `/tech/tpp-standards`, `/tech/lfi-api-hub`, etc. Stable URLs keep those hand-offs working. (Our weekly link-checker watches them and files an issue if any break — so we'll flag drift to you automatically.)

3. **A reciprocal link.**
   A pointer from your site — *"for independent ecosystem tracking and global context → openfinance-os.org"* — completes the loop and sends the right visitors each way.

## Why this is good for the program

An independent, methodology-transparent observatory *strengthens* Al Tareq: when adoption progress comes from a neutral third party rather than the operator's own dashboard, it reads as evidence, not marketing. You get a credible external scorekeeper, a free long-term archive of your own data, and one less thing to build and maintain — while the community gets a clear front door and a place to convene that no single commercial operator owns.
