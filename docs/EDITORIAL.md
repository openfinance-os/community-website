# Editorial Guidelines

These guidelines ensure consistent, high-quality content across Open Finance OS. They apply to all content on the website, including news, events, learn articles, and documentation.

## Content Types

### Official
Content sourced directly from regulators (CBUAE) or official industry bodies.
- Must link to primary source
- Quote accurately without editorializing
- Include publication date
- Label clearly as official/regulatory content

### Milestone
Significant ecosystem achievements and announcements.
- First implementations, major launches
- Regulatory approvals or certifications
- Industry-wide achievements
- Requires verifiable source

### Community
Content from ecosystem participants and community members.
- Bank or TPP announcements
- Integration stories
- Community events
- May include quotes or perspectives

### Global
International Open Banking/Finance news relevant to UAE practitioners.
- UK, EU, Australia, or other jurisdiction updates
- Global standards developments
- Cross-border initiatives
- Contextualize relevance to UAE

### Opinion
Clearly labeled perspectives and analysis.
- Must be marked as "Opinion" or "Analysis"
- Represent author's views, not Open Finance OS position
- Based on factual foundation
- Open to respectful disagreement

## Tone and Voice

### We Are
- **Informative**: Provide useful, accurate information
- **Accessible**: Explain concepts clearly, avoid unnecessary jargon
- **Neutral**: Present facts without advocacy or bias
- **Welcoming**: Make Open Finance approachable to newcomers
- **Honest**: Acknowledge limitations and uncertainties

### We Avoid
- Marketing language or promotional tone
- Taking sides in commercial competition
- Speculation presented as fact
- Sensationalism or clickbait
- Dismissive or gatekeeping attitudes

### Writing Style
- Use plain language where possible
- Define technical terms on first use
- Write for an international English audience
- Keep sentences and paragraphs concise
- Use active voice

## Sourcing Requirements

### Required
All factual claims must be sourced. Acceptable sources include:
- Official regulator publications (CBUAE, etc.)
- Primary announcements from banks/TPPs
- Established news outlets
- Official API documentation
- Published research and reports

### Attribution
- Link to primary sources where available
- Include publication dates
- Quote accurately
- Distinguish between confirmed facts and reported claims

### What We Don't Accept
- Unverified rumors
- Anonymous sources for significant claims
- Social media as sole source (unless from official accounts)
- Paywalled content without summary permission

## What We Don't Publish

### Never
- Confidential or non-public information
- Content that violates privacy or data protection
- Defamatory or libelous claims
- Content that promotes specific commercial products over others
- Anything that could harm the ecosystem or its participants

### Rarely (requires maintainer approval)
- Speculation about unreleased features or timelines
- Criticism of specific organizations
- Content about pending regulatory decisions
- Breaking news without confirmed sources

## Review Process

### Standard Content
1. Submit via GitHub Issue or Pull Request
2. Maintainer reviews for accuracy and guidelines compliance
3. Technical review if needed
4. Publish with appropriate labels

### Sensitive Content
For content that is time-sensitive, potentially controversial, or involves criticism:
1. Flag as "needs review" in submission
2. Multiple maintainer review
3. May reach out to subjects for comment/accuracy
4. Extended review period if needed

### Updates and Corrections
- Errors should be corrected promptly
- Significant corrections noted with "[Updated]" tag
- Factual corrections take priority over style changes

## Content Labels

Apply these labels to help readers understand content context:

| Label | Use When |
|-------|----------|
| `Official` | Sourced from regulators or official bodies |
| `Milestone` | Significant ecosystem achievement |
| `Community` | From ecosystem participants |
| `Global` | International news relevant to UAE |
| `Opinion` | Perspective or analysis piece |
| `Explainer` | Educational content explaining concepts |
| `Event` | Conference, meetup, or gathering |
| `Updated` | Content has been significantly revised |

## Observatory Data

The Observatory pulls data directly from the Raidiam Trust Framework Directory. For Observatory content:
- Data accuracy is as reported by the directory
- We do not editorialize participant status
- Methodology is documented separately
- Questions about data should go to the primary source
