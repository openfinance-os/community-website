This was the inaugural event that launched the OpenFinance-OS community. Over 50 attendees from banks, fintechs, and technology companies came together to discuss the future of Open Finance in the UAE.

## Highlights

- Introduction to the OpenFinance-OS mission and values
- Overview of the Observatory dashboard
- Panel discussion on ecosystem challenges
- Networking and community building

## Key Takeaways

1. Strong interest in community-driven resources
2. Need for better developer documentation
3. Desire for more frequent meetups across emirates
4. Enthusiasm for open source collaboration

Thank you to everyone who attended and helped launch this initiative!
