Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB), one of the UAE's leading Islamic financial institutions, is leading the push into the open finance era as the country implements its groundbreaking Open Finance framework.

ADIB Ventures, the bank's innovation arm, has formed a strategic partnership with Lean Technologies, a MENA-based fintech infrastructure provider, to advance financial innovation. The partnership enables ADIB to offer tailored products and services to fintech companies and corporate clients through Lean's platform, which provides tools for programmatic automated payouts, reconciliation, account verification, and financial operations solutions.

The bank has also launched its API developer portal following UK Open Banking standards, allowing third-party providers such as payment initiators, account aggregators, and other fintechs to deliver new digital services using ADIB's platforms.

This positions ADIB as a key player in the UAE's vision to become a leading open finance economy in the region.
