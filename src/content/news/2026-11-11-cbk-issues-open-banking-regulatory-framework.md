Kuwait's central bank has published the final Open Banking Regulatory Framework after consulting on a June 2025 draft. Licensed banks may share customer data, with the customer's explicit consent, with CBK-authorised account-information and payment-initiation providers, including for a single view of accounts, payments from those accounts, and product comparison.
