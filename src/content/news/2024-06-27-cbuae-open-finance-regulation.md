On 27 June 2024 the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) announced that it had issued the Open Finance Regulation, published in the Official Gazette and brought into effect in phases, establishing the foundation for a new era of financial services innovation in the Emirates.

## Key Highlights

- **Trust Framework Operator:** Al Tareq has been designated as the official trust framework operator
- **Phased Rollout:** Implementation begins with major banks in the first phase
- **API Standards:** Standardized APIs for account information, payments, and confirmation of payee

## What This Means

This regulatory framework positions the UAE as a leader in the GCC region for open finance adoption, following successful models in the UK, EU, and Brazil.

The regulation was superseded by [Circular 03/2025](/news/2025-07-10-cbuae-circular-3-2025) on 10 July 2025; the current text is in the [CBUAE Rulebook](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/open-finance-regulation).
