Experian has announced Experian Cashflow Data Bureau, a US consumer reporting agency operating under the Fair Credit Reporting Act. The company says it combines consumer-permissioned bank-account information, reporting and analytics to support lenders' underwriting decisions.

The announcement concerns a US credit-reporting framework. It does not establish a UAE regulatory approval or a UAE product launch. [Experian announcement](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261006880188/en/Experian-Unveils-Cashflow-Data-Bureau-to-Power-the-Next-Generation-of-Underwriting).
