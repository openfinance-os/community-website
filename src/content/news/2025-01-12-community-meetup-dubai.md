Last week, the OpenFinance-OS community gathered in Dubai for our first in-person meetup, bringing together developers, product managers, and fintech founders to discuss the practical realities of Open Finance implementation.

## Key Takeaways

### 1. Consent UX Matters

Several attendees shared how customer consent flows can make or break adoption. Clear, simple language and transparent data usage explanations led to significantly higher consent rates.

### 2. Error Handling is Critical

Production implementations revealed the importance of robust error handling, especially for:

- Network timeouts
- Token refresh scenarios
- Partial data availability

### 3. Testing in Sandbox vs. Production

The gap between sandbox and production environments was a common challenge. Recommendations included:

- Test with realistic data volumes
- Simulate error conditions early
- Plan for bank-specific variations
