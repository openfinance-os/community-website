MoneyHash has partnered with UAE payment provider VaultsPay. The companies say merchants can connect VaultsPay through MoneyHash's payment-orchestration platform and manage it alongside their other payment providers, avoiding another standalone integration.

VaultsPay's offering includes online and in-person payment capabilities, including cards and digital wallets. [VaultsPay's announcement](https://www.vaultspay.com/) dates the partnership to 6 October 2026; the linked Fintech News report was published on 7 October.
