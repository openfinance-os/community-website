Dubai FinTech Summit takes place on 2–3 November 2026 at Madinat Jumeirah. Michael Ryberg Hartmann from the Open Finance OS community will be there, and if you're reading this after a conversation at the event: welcome.

## What Open Finance OS is

Open Finance OS is an open, community-run resource for people building on UAE Open Finance. It brings together curated [news](/news), the [Observatory](/observatory) that tracks the public trust-framework directory, and the [Commons](/commons): shared tools, references and artifacts such as the [Data Sandbox](/commons/data-sandbox), built for banks, TPPs, developers and regulators alike.

## Why the UAE Open Finance build-out matters

UAE Open Finance uses a centralised model, with a shared API Hub instead of each institution running its own stack. That makes the ecosystem easier to observe and compare, but it also means the details matter: who is registered, what they publish, and what that does and doesn't tell you. As one example, the Observatory's production snapshot of 28 October 2026 showed 12 of 30 directory registrations publishing an active authorization server ([our breakdown](/news/2026-10-29-12-of-30-uae-open-finance-authorization-servers)). Shared, open facts like these help everyone build on the same foundation.
