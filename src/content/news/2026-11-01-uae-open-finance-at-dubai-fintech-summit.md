## Why the UAE matters

UAE Open Finance is supervised by the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE). Customers authorize data sharing and payment initiation through participating providers. Banking and insurance services are mediated by a central API Hub. Al Tareq is the brand a customer sees. Nebras operates that Hub under CBUAE oversight. The [FAQ](/learn/faq) separates the brand, the operator and the regulator.

In the UK and Europe, each bank usually runs its own authorization server. In UAE Open Finance, the API Hub is the OIDC/FAPI authorization server and token issuer. A third party calls the Hub. The Hub routes the request, holds consent and issues the access token. The institution authenticates its customer. The [methodology](/observatory/methodology) is the definition we use: a published issuer is directory metadata. Reachability, certification and traffic are separate questions.

Open Finance OS is an independent community project. It is not a CBUAE, Al Tareq or Nebras site.

## What the directory shows

The [Observatory](/observatory) reads the public trust-framework directory. A registration counts as publishing an authorization server when it has at least one active authorization server with a nonempty issuer.

From the 29 October snapshot, 12:18 UTC, it does. The 1 November snapshot still records 13 of 30. The change is published metadata, not a traffic measure.
