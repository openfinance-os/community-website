The UAE has enacted Federal Decree Law No. 6 of 2025 Regarding the Central Bank Regulation of Financial Institutions and Activities and Insurance Business (the "New CBUAE Law"), marking one of the most significant overhauls of the UAE's financial regulatory framework in over a decade.

## Key Changes

The New CBUAE Law consolidates regulation under a single framework covering:

- Banks and financial institutions
- Payment service providers
- Insurance and related professions
- Virtual asset service providers
- Enabling technology providers

## Open Finance Expansion

The scope of "Licensed Financial Activities" has been expanded to expressly include:

- **Open Finance Services** — now formally recognized in primary legislation
- **Payment Services using Virtual Assets** — bringing crypto under CBUAE oversight
- **DeFi platforms and protocols** — requiring licensing for financial service enablers

## Strengthened Enforcement

Maximum administrative fines increase significantly to AED 1 billion, with new minimum penalties for:

- Unlicensed financial activities
- Promotional activity without authorization
- Non-compliance with regulatory requirements

## Transition Period

Entities newly in scope or entities required to make changes in accordance with the New CBUAE Law have until **16 September 2026** to regularise licensing and compliance.

## Global Significance

The UAE becomes the first country globally to implement a consolidated trust-framework and centralised API hub for Open Finance, enabling a single secure connection to access the entire banking and insurance market.

This law positions the UAE at the forefront of financial regulation globally, particularly in the integration of traditional finance with digital assets and decentralized technologies.
