Lean Technologies has graduated from the Live Proving program under the Central Bank of the UAE's Open Finance Framework, becoming one of the first Third Party Providers (TPPs) to reach this significant milestone.

## Critical Step Forward

Lean's graduation marks a critical step in the UAE's transition from Open Finance preparation to execution. It confirms Lean's technical and operational maturity and positions the company at the forefront of supporting banks and businesses as Open Finance moves into live, regulated operation under the national framework.

## Regulatory Recognition

The In-Principle Approval (IPA) positions Lean as one of the first companies to offer regulated Open Finance services in the UAE, advancing its path toward full licensing. The approval comes as demand grows for faster, more transparent, and personalised financial services.

## Partnership with Banks

Lean is now live and operational with Commercial Bank of Dubai under the AlTareq Open Finance Initiative, delivered in collaboration with fellow CBUAE-licensed TPP Pay10.

## About Lean Technologies

Lean Technologies is the leading financial infrastructure provider in the MENA region, empowering businesses to access financial data and initiate payments through a single, secure platform. Since its founding in 2019, Lean has:

- Supported more than 350 companies
- Processed over $4 billion in transaction volume
- Connected upwards of 2 million accounts
- Facilitated more than 3 million account verifications

This milestone reinforces Lean's position as a key enabler of the UAE's Open Finance ecosystem.
