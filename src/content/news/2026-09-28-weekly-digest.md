Our weekly read on the UAE Open Finance ecosystem, drawn straight from the [Observatory](/observatory) and the [Ecosystem Watch](/observatory/watch/). ## The registry

**12** of **26** production directory registrations publish an active authorization server with an issuer. This is metadata, not verified availability or traffic.

No changes to the production registry this week.

## Ecosystem activity

Reported cumulative and period-based measures (Ecosystem Watch observation, 21 Sept):

- **1,011,740** cumulative API calls across completed months
- Payment success **96%**
- Error rate **23.5%**
- **16** LFIs and **16** TPPs with successful traffic in the source’s last two completed months

These totals and rates cover completed months, not the digest week. This archived observation does not retain its exact traffic window or source cutoff; weekly growth cannot be inferred.
