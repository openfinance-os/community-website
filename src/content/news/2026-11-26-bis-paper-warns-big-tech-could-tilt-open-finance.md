The Bank for International Settlements' Financial Stability Institute published FSI Insights No 79, which argues that open finance lasts only if incentives, liability and performance measurement work, and that big tech participation affects all three. It warns that reciprocity rules limited to financial data can widen big techs' data advantage, that liability should follow actual control rather than licence status, and that AI agents acting through open finance APIs don't fit today's consent and accountability chains. It also recommends that authorities track market concentration and consumer empowerment, not just adoption.
