On 29 October 2026 the Council adopted its first-reading position on the Payment Services Regulation, following Coreper approval the day before. The Council's statement of reasons describes stronger fraud rules and, for open banking, a ban on artificial technical obstacles or discriminatory delays against authorised third parties, plus dedicated data-access interfaces. The rules are not yet in force; Parliament's second reading is still ahead.
