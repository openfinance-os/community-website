Crowe UAE argues that consented data sharing means the firm holding the data may no longer hold the customer relationship, and that institutions should treat Open Finance as a commercial launch, not only a compliance project. It restates the live CBUAE framework: banks and insurers first, later phases for other licensees, TPP licensing, explicit consent, and connections through Al Tareq / Nebras.
