Klarna has published technical documentation for XS2A v2, a new access-to-account API for Klarna Bank AB built on the Berlin Group openFinance (NextGenPSD2) framework. A sandbox was targeted for October 2026 and production for November 2026; Klarna says those dates are planning targets and may change, and that the legacy API will run in parallel during migration.
