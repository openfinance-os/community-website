Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) has successfully gone live on the Al Tareq Open Finance platform, joining the growing list of UAE banks participating in the ecosystem.

## Available APIs

ADIB has launched the following API families:

- **Account Information** — Secure access to account balances and transaction history
- **Payments** — Initiation of domestic payments
- **Confirmation of Payee** — Verification of recipient details before payment

## Technical Details

The implementation follows the UAE Open Finance API specifications and includes:

- OAuth 2.0 based authorization
- FAPI-compliant security profiles
- Real-time consent management

This milestone demonstrates the growing momentum of Open Finance adoption across UAE's banking sector.
