Spare, a leading Open Finance infrastructure provider, has received in-principle approval from the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) to be licensed for Open Finance operations in the UAE.

## First Cohort of Approvals

This milestone places Spare among the first cohort of companies advancing toward full regulatory authorization under the UAE's Open Finance framework.

## Regional Expansion

Spare is already licensed by the Central Bank of Bahrain and operates across Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, UAE, and Kuwait, delivering Open Finance solutions that power:

- **Account aggregation** across multiple financial institutions
- **Open banking payments** with secure initiation
- **Financial data connectivity** for enhanced services

## Platform Capabilities

Spare provides merchants with a secure and unified API platform for financial services, enabling capabilities such as:

- Recurring payments
- Future dated payments
- Account aggregation
- Identity verification
- And more

## Strategic Market Entry

"We see the UAE as a strategically important market for Open Finance, with a clear regulatory framework and strong momentum in digital financial services," said Dalal AlRayes, Co-founder and CEO of Spare.

## About Spare

Headquartered in Saudi Arabia, Spare is an Open Finance infrastructure company that enables businesses to access financial data and payments through a single, secure API. With strong regional regulatory alignment and a growing network of financial institutions, Spare is building the financial connectivity layer of the future.
