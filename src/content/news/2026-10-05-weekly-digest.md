Our weekly read on the UAE Open Finance ecosystem, drawn straight from the [Observatory](/observatory) and the [Ecosystem Watch](/observatory/watch/). Every figure below is computed from public data — the trust-framework directory and the Al Tareq platform's own logs — with our [methodology](/observatory/methodology) in the open.

## The registry

**12** of **27** production directory registrations publish an active authorization server with an issuer. This is published metadata, not verified service availability or traffic.

1 registry change this week:

- **2 Oct** — Standard Chartered joined the production registry (LFI)

## Ecosystem activity

Ecosystem Watch observation: **2026-10-05**; source data through **2026-09-29**.

- **1,011,740** cumulative API calls across **2025-11 through 2026-08**, excluding partial September 2026
- Payment success **96%** and API error rate **23.5%**, across completed months
- **16** LFIs and **16** TPPs with successful traffic in the source's last two completed months

These are cumulative and period-based measures, not activity during the digest week. Changes between observation totals are not reported as weekly growth.
