Our weekly read on the UAE Open Finance ecosystem, drawn straight from the [Observatory](/observatory) and the [Ecosystem Watch](/observatory/watch/). Every figure below is computed from public data — the trust-framework directory and the Al Tareq platform's own logs — with our [methodology](/observatory/methodology) in the open.

## The registry

**14** of **31** production directory registrations publish an active authorization server with an issuer. This is published metadata, not verified service availability or traffic.

2 registry changes this week:

- **4 Nov** — ALAIN AHLIA INSURANCE COMPANY joined the production registry (ISP · LFI)
- **3 Nov** — Standard Chartered added a role (BDSP · BSIP)

## Ecosystem activity

Ecosystem Watch observation: **2026-11-08**; source data through **2026-11-03**.

- **2,259,856** cumulative API calls across 2025-11 through 2026-10 (excluding partial 2026-11)
- Payment success **95.7%** and API error rate **12.7%**, across completed months
- **16** LFIs and **17** TPPs with successful traffic in the source's last two completed months

These are cumulative and period-based measures, not activity during the digest week. Changes between observation totals are not reported as weekly growth.
