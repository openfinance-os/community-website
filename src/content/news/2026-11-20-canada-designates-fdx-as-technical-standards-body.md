Canada's finance minister has designated Financial Data Exchange, acting through an FDX Canada Council, as the technical standards body for the Consumer-Driven Banking framework, and OBSI as the external complaints body. The government says the standard will be interoperable, and that voting on the Canada Council will be limited to Canadian residents and businesses.
