Fintech News UAE reports that Bahrain Fintech Bay's latest ecosystem report sees agentic AI, stablecoins, open finance, and cross-border payments as the main 2027-2030 themes. On AI, it describes agents that could initiate payments or underwrite SMEs through Bahrain's open-banking APIs, once a know-your-agent layer defines identity, permission, and liability. Open finance is framed as a later expansion from banking data into insurance and investments.
