Numou, an ADGM subsidiary, and Comera Financial Holding, part of Royal Group, have signed a partnership agreement to expand the services available through Numou's SME marketplace. The planned offering includes working-capital and supply-chain finance, business-account opening and digital payments.

The agreement expands the platform's planned financing and payment options; it does not establish that every product is already available. [ADGM announcement](https://www.adgm.com/media/announcements/numou-and-comera-financial-holding-partner-to-expand-financing-and-payment-solutions-for-uae-smes).
