According to Fintech News UAE, National Bank of Fujairah (NBF) has added open finance and other new tools to its NBF Direct app, and began rolling those changes out in September 2026. With the open finance feature, customers can share their account data with approved third-party apps, link accounts held at other banks, and view them in one place. The report says people choose which approved firms can see their bank data, while the UAE central bank brings the model in step by step under its UAE Open Finance rules.
