French open-finance firm Powens says its Connector Agentic Factory has already delivered 12 new European connectors, and that build times have fallen from up to three weeks to a few hours, with typical repairs in minutes. Engineers still approve every proposed connector or fix; the company says the AI does not move customer data or payments.
