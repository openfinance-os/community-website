The Central Bank of the UAE has issued Circular No. 03/2025 regarding Open Finance Regulation, which came into force on 10 July 2025. This new circular repeals and replaces the original Open Finance Regulation Circular 7 of 2023.

## Updated Framework

The updated regulation establishes an enhanced framework for the licensing, supervision and operation of Open Finance in the United Arab Emirates, building on the foundation laid by the original 2023 circular.

## Key Provisions

The regulation covers:

- **Licensing requirements** for Open Finance Providers
- **Data Sharing standards** for Account Information Services
- **Service Initiation rules** for Payment Initiation Services
- **Security and consent** management requirements
- **Supervision and compliance** obligations

## Open Finance Provider Categories

Under the updated framework, Open Finance Providers can be licensed for:

- **Account Information Service Provider (AISP)** — accessing and aggregating customer account data
- **Payment Initiation Service Provider (PISP)** — initiating payments on behalf of customers
- **Combined services** — offering both data sharing and payment initiation

## Regulatory Alignment

The updated circular aligns with the broader changes introduced by Federal Decree Law No. 6 of 2025, ensuring consistency across the UAE's financial regulatory framework.

## Compliance Timeline

Financial institutions and Open Finance Providers must ensure compliance with the updated requirements. The regulation works in conjunction with the transition period established by the new CBUAE Law.
