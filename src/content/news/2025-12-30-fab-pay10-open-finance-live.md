Pay10 and First Abu Dhabi Bank (FAB), the UAE's global bank and one of the world's largest and safest financial institutions, have announced they have gone live on Open Finance for retail customers in the UAE.

## Major Milestone

This marks a key milestone in the UAE's Open Finance journey under the Central Bank of the UAE's Open Finance Initiative, AlTareq. FAB is now one of the first banks to launch Open Banking services to customers in the UAE.

## Services Now Available

FAB has enabled selected retail current and savings accounts to connect via Open Finance, allowing secure access to:

- **Pay by Bank** payment initiation
- **Account information sharing** with regulated providers
- **Related Open Finance use cases**

## Successful Production Launch

As part of this go-live phase, Pay10 and FAB have successfully completed live transactions on the production environment, confirming:

- End-to-end connectivity
- Secure data flows
- Operational readiness

This milestone represents the transition from testing to controlled live usage.

## Platform Enablement

Nebras, operating under the oversight of the Central Bank of the UAE, has enabled both FAB and Pay10 on its Open Finance platform. This confirms that all required connectivity, governance, and control standards are in place for participating institutions.

## Industry Perspective

Harry Gill, Founder and Chairman of Pay10, commented: "This 'go live' with First Abu Dhabi Bank represents a meaningful step in enabling secure data access, interoperable payments, and scalable alternative payment innovation. It reflects a market that values execution, trust, and collaboration — principles aligned with the UAE's vision for a resilient digital financial ecosystem."

Pay10 and FAB will now focus on expanding use cases, validating performance at scale, and preparing for wider customer availability in line with the UAE's Open Finance vision.
