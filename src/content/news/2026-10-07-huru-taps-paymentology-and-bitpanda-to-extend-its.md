Huru is extending its account services through partnerships with Paymentology and Bitpanda Enterprise. Paymentology will continue processing Huru's cards and support a planned Jaywan card launch in Q4 2026.

Bitpanda Enterprise announced its Huru partnership on 30 September. It plans to provide crypto trading and custody infrastructure so Huru users can buy, sell and hold crypto within the app, subject to final regulatory approvals. These are planned capabilities rather than confirmation that the new products are live. [Bitpanda announcement](https://group.bitpanda.com/news-and-media/bitpanda-enterprise-and-huru-bring-digital-assets-into-everyday-money-management-in-the-uae/8f659d75-80e5-425a-b4d4-8b380fd45be0).
