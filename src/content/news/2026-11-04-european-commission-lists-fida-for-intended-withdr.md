On 4 November 2026 the Commission published its 2027 work programme. Annex III lists the Financial Data Access proposal (COM(2023) 360) among 18 files it intends to withdraw within six months, citing a stall in talks and little prospect of Council support. The listing is an intention, not the withdrawal itself; that would need a later Commission decision.
