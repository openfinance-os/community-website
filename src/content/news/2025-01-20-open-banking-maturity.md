## Key Observations

### Speed of Adoption

UAE is following a measured approach, prioritizing security and compliance over rapid expansion. This mirrors the UK's early strategy.

### Regulatory Support

The CBUAE's proactive stance provides clear guidelines, reducing uncertainty for participants.

### What's Next

Industry observers expect TPP participation to accelerate in 2025 as sandbox access becomes more streamlined.
