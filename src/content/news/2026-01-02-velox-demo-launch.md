Today we're excited to release **Velox**, an interactive demonstration platform that makes UAE Open Finance accessible to everyone — no sandbox credentials required.

## Why Velox?

Getting started with Open Finance has traditionally required sandbox registration, credential management, and development environment setup. While essential for production integration, this overhead can be a barrier for those who simply want to understand what's possible.

Velox removes that friction entirely. Open your browser, visit the demo, and start exploring immediately.

## What You Can Do

**Explore the Ecosystem**
See real participant data from the Trust Framework Directory. Understand which banks and TPPs are active, their capabilities, and their certification status.

**Understand the APIs**
Walk through Account Information and Payment Initiation API structures. See example requests and responses that mirror the actual Al Tareq specifications.

**Visualize Consent Flows**
Step through the customer authorization journey. Understand how consent is requested, granted, and managed throughout the data-sharing lifecycle.

## Built for the Community

Velox is fully open source under the MIT license. We encourage contributions, whether that's adding new demo scenarios, improving the UI, or translating content.

## Try It Now

Visit [demoapp.openfinance-os.org](https://demoapp.openfinance-os.org) to explore.

When you're ready to build, the [Demo TPP Starter Kit](/commons/demo-tpp-starter-kit) provides a complete working codebase to fork and customize.
