Design Rationale

Symbolism

  • Globe/Network: Represents the interconnected Open Finance ecosystem — banks, TPPs, and regulators working together
  • Orbital rings: Suggest orbits of participation — sandbox, production-ready, and live
  • Four cardinal nodes: Represent the key stakeholders: Banks (LFIs), TPPs, Regulators, and Developers
  • Amber accent dot: The "live" indicator — activity, momentum, and growth in the ecosystem
  • Rounded square container: Modern app icon aesthetic, suggests software/platform
  • Teal primary color: Trust, stability, professionalism — appropriate for financial services
  • Warm amber accent: Approachability, community warmth, activity

Usage Guidelines

✓ Do
